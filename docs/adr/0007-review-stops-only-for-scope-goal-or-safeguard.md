# A review goes to the maintainer only for scope, the goal or a required safeguard

Supersedes [0004](0004-disputed-findings-follow-the-general-test.md), which sent a disputed review finding to the maintainer whenever fixing or leaving it changed what they get, pay or risk. Now a disputed finding goes to them only when it changes the agreed scope, the goal or a required safeguard, and a review stops for them only when the goal or a required safeguard cannot be met without a scope change, or the round budget with its extra round is spent; every other finding and stop is settled and logged in the pull request. This follows the maintainer's choice that, after agreement, work stops for them only on four triggers (the goal or a required safeguard cannot be met without a scope change, a round budget or eval or cost cap is spent, a block only they can clear, or an agreed manual check is due), and that everything else is settled and logged.
