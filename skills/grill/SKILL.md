---
name: grill
description: "Interviewing the maintainer about a plan, decision or idea, one clickable question at a time, until every decision that is theirs is agreed. Use when the maintainer types /mentat:grill or asks to be grilled (\"grill me\"). Claude Code starts it on its own only after the maintainer agrees to an offer of a grill."
---

The maintainer wants to be questioned about a plan, decision or idea until the two of you agree on it. If they typed text after the command, grill them on that; otherwise on the request in hand. This runs once: do it, then normal work resumes.

1. Finish the step in flight first. Never interrupt a running builder, review or test run. Then change nothing while the grill runs: no edits, no commits, no GitHub writes. Reading is fine.
2. Read before asking: the code, the files, the backlog item and what the maintainer has said this session. Lay the subject out as a tree of decisions, where settling one decision opens the ones that depend on it.
3. Ask the maintainer only decisions that are theirs: what to build, its scope, cost and order, and a disagreement that blocks. Decide implementation questions yourself, as `rules.md` "What goes to the maintainer" says. A fact is never a question: look it up in the code, the files or a tool (a subagent for a long search) before the question that needs it.
4. Ask one decision per `AskUserQuestion` prompt, in the shape `rules.md` "How it reaches them" gives, with at most one line of text before it. Never put several questions in one message or one prompt. Every prompt also has a "Stop here" option, last, so it has at most three others; never drop it to fit another. Without the prompt, ask one decision in the message text, with "Stop here" among the answers and no tool call after it.
5. Pick each next question from the answers so far: an answer settles its decision and opens the ones that hang on it, and an answer typed in Other can reshape the tree. A changed answer, one reopened through "Change something" included, also reopens the decisions that depended on it, so no stale choice is listed. Ask nothing whose answer depends on a decision still open, and never ask a decision already answered, in the grill or earlier in the session.
6. Stopping: the "Stop here" option, or Other text saying to stop, ends the grill at once. Reply with two short lists: the decisions agreed so far, and those still open. None of the open ones counts as agreed.
7. Ending: when no decision of the maintainer's is open, ask for confirmation in one `AskUserQuestion` prompt whose question holds a short list of what was agreed, with the options "Yes, that's it" (Recommended; ends the grill, nothing is built yet), "Change something" (reopens the decision the maintainer names, in Other or a follow-up) and "Stop here", last, which ends the grill as step 6 says: the decisions agreed so far stay agreed and the list is not confirmed. Without the prompt, give the list in the message text, with "Stop here" among the answers and no tool call after it. Act on nothing until they confirm. Confirming the list is not agreement to build: a change still goes through `mentat:propose` (weigh, the proposal and the agreement), and the decisions answered here count as answered there and are not asked again; the weigh's file holds the grill's record (below).
8. Second opinion: after "Yes, that's it", and only when the agreed list will not lead to a change in this repository (a change here goes through `mentat:propose`, whose weigh is its second opinion), ask once, in its own `AskUserQuestion` prompt, whether to send the grill's record to Codex and Grok for a second opinion. Options: "Get a second opinion" (Recommended; the record goes to OpenAI and xAI, a few minutes) and "No" (ends here); the question says outside views may catch a framing or an option the grill missed. "No" ends it, so this prompt has no "Stop here". Without the prompt, ask in the message text, with no tool call after it. Make no offer after "Stop here".
9. If the maintainer accepts, read the consult skill's file (`../consult/SKILL.md` from this skill's folder; the Skill tool cannot call it) and follow its steps 1 to 4 rather than running `mentat consult` directly. The question is the grill's subject, its record and a request to judge the questions' framing, any missing options and the decisions reached, so nothing is asked for. The record goes in whole, though it holds Claude Code's questions and options; its stop for anything private holds, Claude Code's own view is its step 2 line, written separately, and its step 4 sets the reply. A consult is not a weigh or a review, and confirming is still not agreement to build.

The grill's record: each question as finally asked, its options, the one marked recommended, and the maintainer's answer. Leave out a question answered "Stop here", and the earlier form of one reopened or replaced; only each decision's final asking stays.
