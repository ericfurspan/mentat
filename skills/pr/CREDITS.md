# Credits

Adapted from the `pr` skill in [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) (`skills/engineering/pr/SKILL.md`): its Summary, Evidence and Merge danger sections. Mentat's Test plan and requirement table come from Evidence, and its Risks from Merge danger, with plain words on whether a revert undoes the change in place of its one-way and two-way door framing.

Matt Pocock's `pr` skill itself credits Dex Horthy's [`show-me`](https://github.com/humanlayer/skills/blob/main/plugins/show-me/skills/show-me/SKILL.md) skill (Humanlayer).

Step 3 (ask the maintainer when the change's intent cannot be stated in one sentence) comes from Jeffallan's `code-reviewer` skill ([code-reviewer](https://jeffallan.github.io/claude-skills/skills/quality/code-reviewer/)); `skills/review/CREDITS.md` has the details.

Changes for Mentat: the Summary's opening sentence states the intent, because Codex and Grok review against the body; a Status line follows it; Risks names every item to read before merging; See it appears only when there is something to try; the review record, the requirement table and the models that built it are folded under Review details; repository templates and glossaries come first; the menu of visuals is left out.
