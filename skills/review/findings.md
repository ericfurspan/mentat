# Findings

Read this when sorting review findings, and again after a compaction.

Claude Code sorts every review finding, from Claude's local review and every outside reviewer alike. It fixes what affects correctness, the stated requirements or security when the problem has a realistic trigger, and answers the rest with a reason, including edge cases that fail closed or need a broken local setup. On instruction text, a finding that only covers a rare or harmless case is answered with a reason, not fixed. A defect Claude Code finds or agrees is real is fixed in the pull request, at any round before the last within the limit, except at a pending stop (`stop.md`), where fixes wait until it is settled or answered; it goes to the maintainer only when the fix would change the agreed scope or, at the round limit, the head cannot be Ready, never into a follow-up item on its own, except one left at the round limit on a head that may be Ready; at the round limit, both come only after the extra round where `stop.md` "Round limit" allows one. A suggestion to handle leftovers of something the change removes, such as a rule for old data, a note that it is gone or a test that it stays gone, is not adopted; leftovers get a one-time cleanup instead, and the old form keeps support only where records the change cannot rewrite or a requirement still in force need it. Non-defect improvements become cheap fixes or follow-ups. A reviewer's blocking or non-blocking mark is a claim, and Claude Code's sorting decides each finding; one it disagrees with goes to the maintainer only when fixing or leaving it changes the agreed scope, the goal or a required safeguard, and their answer to leave it settles it, its Review details line recording it as settled by the maintainer. Claude Code settles every other disputed finding itself and logs it, with its reason, in Review details.

A finding raised again with no new evidence after its answer was recorded in Review details, unless it was settled by the maintainer and the text it was about changed since, by itself makes no stop pending (`stop.md`); it is answered by pointing to that line when it also names no specific error in that answer, and sorted on whether the error holds when it names one. One settled by the maintainer, raised again with no new evidence and the text it was about unchanged, counts as answered: it does not keep the head from Ready, does not go to the maintainer again, and, when marked blocking, keeps its `Risk:` line under Decisions and Risks as a blocking finding answered instead of fixed. Raised again with new evidence, or a settled one after the text it was about changed, it is sorted like any finding.

A fix to instruction text (skills, agent prompts, reviewer criteria, hook messages, and text every session loads, such as `rules.md` or the maintainer's own instructions) replaces or tightens existing text before it adds any: the fix names the text it replaces or tightens, and adds new text only where no existing text covers the case. A finding caused by the previous round's fix reworks that fix first, adding text only where a rework cannot cover it.
