# Credits

Adapted from the `retro` skill in [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) (`skills/engineering/retro/SKILL.md`): the categories to look through (navigation, automated checks, steering files, tool economy, instructions that change nothing, information access) and the "use when" cues.

Changes for Mentat: it looks at Mentat's own workflow too; it changes nothing and replies once, ranked by severity with evidence from the session; a mistake a check could catch gets a check, not a rule; it ends by offering to open issues for the candidates the maintainer picks; the reviewer-agent standards file, the writing style skill and the implementation-versus-review notes are left out.
